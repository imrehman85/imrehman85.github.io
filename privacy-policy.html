Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Doc Master: PDF Kit ("DocMaster", "the app", "we", "our") handles information. The app is a PDF scanner, editor and converter for Android, published on Google Play by the developer listed on the app's store page.

In short: your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share them. The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Your Documents and Files

Everything you scan, create, edit, convert, sign, merge, split, compress or password-protect is processed on your device and saved in the app's local storage. We do not upload, collect, read or store your documents, images or extracted text on any server. Files leave your device only when you choose to share or export them, for example through the Android share sheet. In that case the app or service you choose handles them under its own privacy policy.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan documents when you start a scan. Images stay on your device.
  • Files you pick: when you choose a PDF or image with the system file picker, the app reads only that file to process it locally.
  • Internet: used to load ads, to check Google Play for app updates, and to open links you tap (such as the Play Store page).

3. Text Recognition (OCR)

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit's on-device model. Images and recognized text are processed locally and are not sent to us or to Google for recognition.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, full-screen (interstitial and app open) and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud on these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • The device's Advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate general location (country or region)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, OS version, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as impressions, clicks and video views, and diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. It is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalized ads and consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad Privacy Choices. Users in certain US states can also opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same Google consent tools where they apply.

Controlling the Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads on some devices.

Ad-free reward. If you choose to watch a rewarded video in Settings → Ads, ads are hidden for one hour. The app stores only the time the reward ends, on your device.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

The app keeps a small amount of information locally, such as your theme, default PDF quality, recent files list and ad-free reward end time. You can clear the recent history and temporary files in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes all of this data along with the app's stored files.

6. Other Google Services

  • Google Play In-App Updates: checks Google Play for newer versions of the app.
  • Google Play Services: required by the ads SDK and the document scanner.

These services are provided by Google and covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security

Your files are protected by your device's security, such as screen lock and storage encryption. You can also protect PDFs with a password using the app's AES encryption feature.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through ads in the app, please contact us and we will help you request its removal from Google.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, most requests are handled directly by Google for ad data. You can manage it through your ad privacy choices in the app, your device's ad settings, and Google My Ad Center. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete your data, which you can exercise with Google or by contacting us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are shown by updating the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
